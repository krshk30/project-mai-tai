## Result

None of the six requested target/floor combinations was profitable in this sample. Changing only
the exit bracket also did not materially reduce trading: each policy produced 127-129 trades from
26 active symbol-sessions. There is no evidence here for a "little trade, only winning trade"
configuration.

The zero floor is not breakeven in executable-price terms. Entry is at the ask and exit is at the
bid, so it closed almost immediately for a median loss of 0.6163%. The best mean return among
policies that allowed winners was +1% / -1%, but it still averaged -0.8232% per trade with a 19.7%
win rate. The highest win rate was +1% / -2% at 35.7%, but its average was -1.0641% per trade.

## Population And Method

The population came from stored MACD momentum scanner events. A symbol was eligible only between
its real-time `CONFIRM` and `FADE`/`RETENTION_DROP`; carry-forward confirms were excluded using the
stored event/creation-time check. No symbol was selected from price data alone.

| Session | Confirmed symbols | ATR-gradeable | Active with entries | Sparse Schwab bars |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-08-24 | 13 | 6 | 5 | 7 |
| 2026-08-25 | 20 | 12 | 4 | 8 |
| 2026-08-26 | 21 | 10 | 4 | 11 |
| 2026-08-27 | 19 | 9 | 3 | 10 |
| 2026-08-28 | 15 | 5 | 1 | 10 |
| 2026-08-31 | 18 | 13 | 5 | 5 |
| 2026-09-01 | 14 | 9 | 4 | 5 |
| **Total** | **120** | **64** | **26** | **56** |

The 56 sparse symbol-sessions had fewer than eight stored Schwab one-minute bars and were excluded,
not treated as no-trade passes. The 64 gradeable symbol-sessions had 5,728,712 dense exit quotes.

Entries were generated by replaying the current live `SchwabV2Strategy`, including its ATR flip,
first and reclaim slot claims, resting and reactive execution paths, watch-start cap, and ATR
bar-adjacency guard. Schwab bars and quotes drove strategy decisions. One filled strategy signal was
counted once; Webull fan-out was disabled because a second venue leg is not a second ATR opportunity.

Each counterfactual entry filled at the observed Schwab ask. A target filled at its exact +1% or +2%
price when the dense captured bid first reached it. A floor triggered when bid reached 0%, -1%, or
-2%, then filled at the next captured bid. A modeled close was fed back to the live state machine so
that a later reclaim could occur. Scanner removal blocked new entries but did not force-close an
open trade. Any remaining position was closed at the final bid before 16:00 ET.

## Validation And Limits

- Zero entries occurred outside a real-time scanner window.
- Zero entries occurred outside 07:00-16:00 ET.
- Zero duplicate policy/symbol/entry timestamps occurred.
- Every target exit filled exactly at its stated target.
- Every floor exit used a quote strictly later than its trigger quote.
- The focused and related replay/backtest suites passed: 99 tests.
- The model includes no commissions, fees, queue position, partial fills, rejects, or order latency.
- Exact target fills and next-captured-bid floor fills are optimistic compared with live execution.
- Seven sessions, 64 gradeable symbol-sessions, and 26 active symbol-sessions are evidence, not proof.
- This branch changes no live strategy, service, configuration, or deployment behavior.
