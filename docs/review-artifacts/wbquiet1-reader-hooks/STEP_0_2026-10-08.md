## Registry and truthful joins

The observer retains a service-local, per-account registry of committed generations:
at most 16 accounts and 16 generations per account. Entries expire 60 seconds after
the observed persistence transaction returns. The original 48-entry in-pass reader
bound remains. Registry/adapter-cache locks are nonblocking; no new task/log queue
or DB/HTTP operation is created by a hook.

Each registry entry records actual process PID, sync pass ID, account, local read
invocation ID, adapter-call count, acquisition/commit elapsed times and acquisition
generation. A source acquisition stamp is attributed only when a bounded, nonempty
returned list contains the same immutable snapshot objects as that account's cache,
under its existing cache lock. Empty lists, equal-valued copies, contention, missing
cache and mismatches are UNMEASURED, never assigned a fresh pass-derived generation.
The local read ID is explicitly distinct from the source acquisition stamp.

Later `[WBQUIET-READER]` notes join those exact identifiers without changing or
re-emitting the published `[WBQUIET-SHADOW]` receipt. They distinguish overlapping
committed passes from demonstrated cache-object source reuse. Snapshot `as_of`
metadata and exact-entry renewal bindings are observations, not wire IDs. Counts
are real instrumented adapter invocations, including unreadable completions;
cache returns/backoff mean they are not wire-pull counts or savings.

Provider comes from already-constructed account routing; unknown routing remains
UNMEASURED. ORB/EOD is explicitly Schwab-scoped and excluded from Webull cadence,
even if routing is misconfigured. Native bracket renewal is not a position pull.
Other OMS/external-reader coverage remains PARTIAL. Existing SHADOW's external-reader
UNMEASURED field is preserved. No complete downstream equivalence is asserted.

Observer/logging exceptions do not escape reader hooks. Dropped/overflowed or
unpublished observations cannot establish complete generation evidence. Silent
absence outside a window, absent observer and canceled reads are missing evidence,
not zero reads. The existing logging sink is synchronous; registry contention does
not wait, but this lane does not certify arbitrary logging-handler latency.
