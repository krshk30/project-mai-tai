## Code and proposed-fix assessment

_rpg_begin_cancel removes a proven local PA1 hold; _rpg_retire_price_wait
removes each held replacement before submitting another attempt. The new
replacement can still be outside the same unchanged 8% guard. PA1's pre-check
then deletes it at the retry cap, so the job becomes refused with no remaining
price-wait owner. Subsequent Schwab reprices cannot recover that mirror from
the shared rest latch.

Keep the slot's distance wait while a current authorization is outside 8%.
Only the hand-off's serial dispatcher may retry an RPG-owned wait, using fresh
market evidence and the current strategy authorization. Retain all fill,
unknown-dispatch, account, generation, slot, segment, window and cancellation
guards; don't change the band or the 8% distance. A cap bounds immediate work,
not ownership; a later closed-bar reprice can re-evaluate the still-held leg.
Normal acceptance/fill, cancellation, segment end and window end retire it.
NFQ freshness waiting remains separate and must still compose. No saved buy
may be dispatched outside the entry window or replayed after a confirmed fill.
