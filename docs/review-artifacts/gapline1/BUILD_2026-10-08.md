## Released scope

AGREE with the operator's October 8 carry scope. The earlier Step 0 STOP concerned
the claim that every named hole was already backfilled, not permission to carry
the line. This build preserves the pre-hold indicator on both initial and
recovery gaps. The flag defaults OFF; the catalog expects ON at reviewed install.
Session-anchor resets, the detector and ten-contiguous-live-bar wait are unchanged.

The existing LINE=CHART worker remains the entry-admission authority. During an
unresolved repair, state-only mathematics advances the carry without issuing an
entry or exit observation or resetting consumed slots. Complete source repair
uses the existing `asyncio.to_thread(fetch_session_history)` and bounded stored/
math worker. GAPLINE1 performs no tick-path fetch or database operation.

One event is requested at the first post-hold bar, independently for a recovery
gap. A newly arriving interior candle can refresh the source proof; duplicate
candles and ordinary live tails cannot. Pending requests coalesce by symbol and
the identity/revision fence rejects an obsolete response. A retained exit-covered
symbol can repair its line, but buy admission remains scanner-watchlist-only.
