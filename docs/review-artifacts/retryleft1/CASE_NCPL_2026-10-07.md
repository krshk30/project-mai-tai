## 2. Why Schwab filled and Webull did not, at the same 1.64

Our own tape (Schwab stream, `market_trade_ticks`) shows 8 NCPL prints in the 15:08 window with a high of 1.63 — no trade at 1.64. Our quote capture holds one NCPL quote in that window (ask 1.63; quotes publish only on NBBO change). So:
- Schwab's stop triggered on something we did not capture — most likely a brief quote touch at 1.64 — and filled at the then-offer 1.63 under the 1.65 limit.
- Webull triggers a stop on the last trade; no trade reached 1.64, so its order kept waiting.
Trigger source on Schwab is NOT proven from our data. It is the known broker-asymmetry family (different trigger rules at the two brokers). It cuts both ways on other days.

## 3. Why the Webull order stayed alive — the defect (RETRYLEFT1)

After a close in the segment the bot has one rule: **do not place a new buy** (RETRYOFF1, operator 10-06). It has **no step that cancels the sibling waiting buy that already exists** on the other broker. The only things that would have removed it are a line move (reprice → cancel + replace) or a flip (cancel). Neither happened: the line held and never flipped. So the order sat at the broker as a live second entry for the segment. Had NCPL crossed 1.64 again, Webull would have bought 182 shares in a segment whose trade was already closed — exactly what the operator ruled out.

Call: **REAL FAILURE — a missing step, not a failed cancel and not a failed reprice.**

## 6. The fix (lane RETRYLEFT1, codex builds, claude-1 reviews)

**Operator's card (plain English):** When the bot's trade in a segment has closed and no second buy is allowed, every waiting buy for that segment is cancelled on both brokers, right away — pre-market soft rest and regular-hours broker order alike.

**Exact change:** at the point where v2 logs `[V2-FLIP-OWNER-RETRY] … action=held reason=retry_budget_exhausted`, emit a cancel intent for every live resting order of that symbol/segment on both accounts (the same cancel the bot already uses for `reason=flip_no_fill` / `watchlist-removed`), log `[V2-RETRY-LEFTOVER-CANCEL] symbol account qty reason=retry_budget_exhausted`, and confirm the cancel receipts through the existing CLEARWAIT1 barrier.

**Scenarios covered:** one leg filled + closed (confirmation exit, target, stop, flip exit) with the other leg's rest live — RTH broker rest (Webull or Schwab), pre-market soft rest (software, both legs), restart during the hold. **Not covered / untouched:** both legs filled (no leftover), the sibling cancelled already by a flip/reprice, KEEPREST1 (a waiting buy that survives a BUY flip WITHOUT a fill — no close happened, so this change must not fire there).

**Must not break:** KEEPREST1, CLEARWAIT1 (removal clears a wait), one trade per segment, RESERVE1 bracket release ordering, the policy-reject path (no sibling exists), the manual-stop lever (a hand cancel stays a manual stop).

**Replay:** NCPL 10-07 19:01→19:33 UTC and APUS 09-24 16:26 ET from the stored logs; both must end with the sibling cancelled within one bar of the close.
